🔍 Reconnaissance

Gathering information about the target system and identifying potential vulnerabilities.

Techniques: Network scanning, service enumeration, OSINT

🚪 Initial Access

Gaining initial foothold in the target environment through exploitation or social engineering.

Vectors: Phishing, vulnerable services, stolen credentials
Metasploit Cobalt Strike Social Engineering
3
💥 Exploitation

Executing code to leverage vulnerabilities and gain further control of the system.

Activities: Privilege escalation, credential dumping, persistence establishment
Mimikatz PowerSploit BloodHound Hashcat
4
🔄 Lateral Movement

Moving through the network to access additional systems and higher-value targets.

Methods: Pass-the-hash, RDP, WMI, SSH tunneling
pwdump CrackMapExec PsExec RDP
5
⚙️ Persistence & Command & Control

Maintaining access and establishing communication channels with compromised systems.

Techniques: Scheduled tasks, service installation, registry modifications
Empire Sliver Web shells DNS tunneling
6
📤 Exfiltration

Stealing and transferring data from the target environment to attacker-controlled systems.

Methods: DNS tunneling, HTTPS, FTP, cloud storage
